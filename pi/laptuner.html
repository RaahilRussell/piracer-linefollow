Race day

  1. Put the car on the line and check the camera: the orange line must be tracked (Straight or Curve top left).
  2. Tap First slow lap, then Full Auto. Watch one lap.
  3. Tap Race. If it runs wide, tap Safe race.
  4. Save on car keeps the settings after a restart. STOP or Space returns to Manual at zero throttle.

Modes

Manual: you drive with the gamepad. Pilot steers: the pilot steers, you give throttle. Full Auto: the pilot steers and sets speed; gamepad sticks take over instantly, Y is emergency stop.

How it steers

Pure pursuit (default) projects the line onto the ground, shifts it to where the car should ride, and steers the arc to a point ahead. PD steering (fallback) steers in proportion to how far the line sits from its target on one image row.

Lap tuner

  • Drives laps, scoring each straight and curve (tracking, weave, lag). After clean laps it speeds up one step on straights, half a step on curves.
  • A failing section drops back to its last clean speed minus a safety margin and is held; the other keeps climbing.
  • If the line is lost the car stops. Put it back and pick Full Auto to continue. Save on car when it finishes.
