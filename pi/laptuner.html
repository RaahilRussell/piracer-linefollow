How this works

The goal

Follow the left orange line around the stage track faster than a human lap (about 3.4 s). The camera finds the line's inside edge; the pilot steers to keep it at the target position.

Two kinds of track

The pilot measures how much the line bends ahead and labels each moment Straight or Curve. On straights it runs at straight throttle; entering a curve it brakes to curve throttle.

How it steers

Pure pursuit (the default) turns the line it sees into points on the ground, moves them sideways to where the car should ride, picks the point a lookahead distance ahead and steers the arc that reaches it. PD steering (the fallback) steers in proportion to how far the line sits from its target on one image row; KP and KD only matter there, or when the PD share is above 0.

The speed ramp

  • Start first puts the race values on the car and checks them. It reports the lap time and the race total (an estimate until laps have been timed).
  • It drives laps and scores each straight and each curve: is the line tracked, is it weaving, is the steering lagging.
  • After two clean laps in a row at a speed (adjustable), straight throttle goes up one step and curve throttle half a step.
  • If a section weaves or lags it adjusts that section's KP/KD and checks again.
  • When a section fails (line lost, health check fails, or you press Curve too fast / Straight too fast) it drops back to the last clean speed, minus a one-step safety margin (adjustable), and holds it there. The other section keeps climbing.
  • If the line is lost the car stops. Put it back and pick Full Auto; it continues from the next step.
  • It finishes when both sections are held or hit the Full Auto limit. Then press Save on car.

What would make it faster

  • Track measurements (lane width, curve radii, straight lengths) let the curve detector and feedforward use real numbers instead of guesses.
  • Measured steering angle at full lock makes feedforward accurate, so the PD loop only fixes small errors.
  • Timed runs at a few throttles would replace the assumed speed table, so the lap estimate and the speed-scaled lookahead use real speeds.
